# Visual Preview and UI/UX Flow

## Visual Style
FireStorm Royale aims for an **Ultra HD Realistic** aesthetic, similar to top-tier AAA survival shooters.

### 1. Environment (Unity 3D)
- **High-Fidelity Textures**: Realistic terrain, foliage, and urban structures.
- **Dynamic Lighting**: Real-time shadows, global illumination, and a full day/night cycle.
- **Weather Effects**: Dynamic rain, fog, and lightning that affect visibility and gameplay.

### 2. Characters & Animations
- **Highly Detailed Models**: Customizable outfits with physics-based cloth simulation.
- **Motion Captured Animations**: Fluid movement for running, jumping, crawling, and combat.
- **Realistic Gunplay**: Detailed weapon models with authentic reload animations and recoil.

### 3. User Interface (Flutter)
- **Modern Sleek Design**: Dark theme with orange/gold accents (as seen in the launcher).
- **HUD (In-Game)**:
    - Minimalist health and armor bars.
    - Mini-map with safe zone indicators.
    - Contextual action buttons (Pick up, Reload, Enter Vehicle).
- **Main Menu**: High-quality 3D character preview in the background with interactive tabs for Inventory, Battle Pass, and Social.

## UI/UX Flow
1. **Splash Screen**: FireStorm Royale logo with loading bar.
2. **Login/Signup**: Social login (Google, Facebook) via Firebase.
3. **Main Lobby**: Character view, friends list, and "Matchmaking" button.
4. **Loading Screen**: Map overview and gameplay tips.
5. **Gameplay**: 100-player Battle Royale action.
6. **Post-Match**: Match stats, rank progression, and reward collection.
